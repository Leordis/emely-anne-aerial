AERIAL PERFORMANCE

When She Leaves the Ground,
the Room Holds Its Breath.

Aerial hoop. Aerial silks. Two disciplines, one artist, infinite possibilities. Emily's aerial work is built on years of technical training and stage performance — the kind of precision that makes the impossible look effortless.

Aerial Hoop (Lyra)

The steel hoop suspended above your guests. A discipline that combines the grace of ballet with the strength of an acrobat. Performable indoors or outdoors, at intimate dinners or grand galas. The visual that gets shared from your event.

Aerial Silks

Two lengths of fabric, floor to rigging, wrapped around a performer who moves through them like water. Aerial silks create atmosphere as much as spectacle — the right lighting, the right music, the right artist transform any venue into something cinematic.

A rigging point rated for appropriate load is required. Emily provides a complete technical rider upon booking. Most professional venues in Orlando and Central Florida are fully compatible.

ABOUT EMILY

The Artist.
The Athlete.
The Professional.

Emily Anne is a professional aerial artist, dancer, and live performer based in Orlando, Florida. She has performed at corporate events, luxury weddings, private celebrations, and festivals throughout Central Florida — bringing a level of technical precision and genuine artistry to every stage she steps onto.

Before performing professionally, Emily built her foundation as a certified personal trainer and group fitness instructor — a background that gives her a level of body awareness, physical control, and movement intelligence that separates her from performers who never trained this way. Her athleticism isn't decorative. It's structural. It's the reason her aerial work is as clean as it is.

Emily doesn't outsource the details of her performances. She choreographs them. She designs the costume. She selects the music. She coordinates with your venue. She shows up on time, performs at the level she promised, and leaves your event better than she found it. Every time.

Everything You Need
Before You Book.

Emily is based in Orlando, Florida, and performs throughout Central Florida, the Greater Orlando area, Tampa Bay, and Miami. Destination events — both domestic and international — are welcome. Travel and accommodation fees apply for events outside Central Florida and are discussed transparently at the time of inquiry.

Aerial performances require a structural rigging point — typically a beam, truss, or purpose-built aerial point — rated for appropriate load-bearing capacity. Emily provides a complete technical rider with all specifications immediately upon booking confirmation. The majority of professional venues in Orlando and Central Florida are fully compatible. For venues that aren't, Emily's team works directly with your coordinator to identify solutions before the event.

Emily's calendar fills quickly — particularly for Friday and Saturday dates between October and April. For corporate events and private celebrations, a minimum of 6–8 weeks' notice is recommended. For weddings, 3–6 months is ideal. That said, last-minute availability occasionally exists — reach out and ask.

Every performance Emily delivers is custom-built for that specific event. Costume selection, music, choreography, entry, duration, and performance style are all designed around your theme, aesthetic, and vision. A private consultation call — included with every booking — is where this process begins.

Every booking includes: a personal consultation call with Emily, fully custom choreography and costume coordination, complete technical coordination with your venue team, the live performance, and follow-up communication. Pricing varies based on event type, performance duration, number of disciplines, and travel requirements. Contact us for a personalized quote — Emily responds to every inquiry personally.

Yes. Emily carries professional performer liability insurance and holds all relevant certifications for her disciplines. Full documentation is available upon request and is routinely provided for corporate clients and venue contracts.
